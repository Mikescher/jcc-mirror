An empty target is the chosen remote's host, which is the publisher's address. A reply proves the rootserver forwards between spokes, which is the single most likely reason for the whole thing to fail and is not a jcc-mirror bug: the server's peer entries have to cover each client's address, and it has to have IP forwarding on.

A bounded read of one file on the chosen remote, timed end to end and then discarded. It is capped at 256 MiB, and the bandwidth schedule applies to it like any other transfer — a probe inside a capped window measures the cap, not the link.
